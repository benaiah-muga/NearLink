# Contributor Covenant Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We are committed to making participation in NearLink welcoming and respectful for everyone, regardless of experience, identity, background, or viewpoint.

## Our standards

Examples of behavior that contributes to a positive environment include:

- Being respectful and constructive.
- Giving and accepting feedback with care.
- Taking responsibility for mistakes and learning from them.
- Focusing on what is best for the project and its community.

Unacceptable behavior includes harassment, personal attacks, discrimination, publishing another person's private information, and sustained disruption of project spaces.

## Enforcement

Report conduct concerns privately to the project maintainers through the contact route shown in the repository profile. Include what happened, when it happened, and any relevant links. Maintainers will review reports promptly and keep them confidential to the extent practical.

Maintainers may remove content, restrict participation, or take other actions needed to protect the community. A person affected by an enforcement decision may request a review from a maintainer who was not involved in the original decision.

## Scope

This code applies in project spaces and when a person is representing NearLink in public spaces.
